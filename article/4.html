    Another week, another update. Let’s go.

    Where are we? Revenue: 0 Pilot clients: 2 Demos booked this week: 1

    This week, I read through Founding Sales and learned about the art of cold calling, cold emailing and all that fun jazz. Whenever I could, I have been giving it a shot to message people.

    I have got mostly fuck offs and we are not interested please do not call, but one guy actually checked it out and loved what I did so that’s going for me. We are now friends :) I will continue to push and hit my activity numbers cause honestly, that’s all I can do.

    I am still not used to the rejection side of things and I do have to keep reminding myself I am “engineering” a process here. I think calling it that means I don’t take it personally. I do genuinely want to help out in this space and I have some idea of what people in this space are like: burned out, underpaid (especially in the UK) and frankly living day to day - so trying to be considerate.

    Another thing I have been focussing is making sure I am not just spending all day doing outreach and getting rejected. I think that leads to burnout. So, I am trying to also work out some way for me to actually enjoy the process, whether that is working on something cool, novel or fun. I do this because I genuinely like to do tech and I don’t think I make enough time for me to actually do that. I haven’t figured out how this works or fits in… part of me says that it’s not the right time to even do that especially as I am looking to survive here but another part says that I need that release valve.

    I have been working on my figure drawing more. Going over Proko’s structure videos. I have found that his approach is far more digestable than Loomis throwing the entire body anatomy at you. It does help with the art work. I don’t really think about it as I am doing it but when I take a step back, I do apply what I learn and then go back in.

    Vibecheck has finally got enterprise ready so that’s going pretty good. I do need to spice it up for the socials but I can do that next week.
