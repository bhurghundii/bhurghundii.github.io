    I thought that I’d want to share whatever the fuck I did this week, following the footsteps of my old uni and CapitalOne senior Jamie Tanna (yo! We did speak and run into each other a few times. I don’t think we had a relationship beyond that but your work inspires me.)

    To be totally clear, my career goal atm is simple: can I make £500PM recurring revenue off my own software? I feel this is the Asylum Demon of going indie.

    In that vein, I’m learning and building in public cause Claude suggested I do that to help build good habits and document my learnings. I got time so let’s do this.

    Where are we?<br /> Revenue: 0

    The good news is that I have secured two pilots and have pivoted my idea cause of those conversations quite substantially. After getting rejected a billion times trying to sell AI to families, I figured that a far better use of my time was to go bigger and try and automate a workflow within the care ecosystem. This seems to have worked: I reached out to a few other folks and have been able to secure more demos and activity.

    It feels nice to finally get some responses. I am a bit wary though - could be a false start. I’ve been reading founding sales by Pete Kazanjy which has been incredible cause it has done two things: tell me I’m on the right track and tell me I need to double down on my activity.

    Some lovely ideas there I’m going to implement over the next week. I really love the work Albert does and it’s a little bit of a joy to see the existing clients interact with it and feel at ease from it. Its a good feeling.

    Aside from that, arts been going pretty good. I’ve been heavily focussed on improving my technique and seeing improvements. I did a whole bunch of colour charts exploring a brand new palette mix of Schmidd and Zorn. Life class has been really solid for me too - I can see a marked improvement in my work.

    In terms of health, been losing weight and feeling good about it. A lot of people say I don’t need to lose weight but when you’re as small as me, you can feel those extra kilos. Besides, I am looking for an athletic fit more than anything. I think that’s feasible. Mentally, much better now that I’ve been working on projects I care about a lot more.

    I remember planning to quit and go travel the world. This is a weird thing considering I’ve spent my childhood running around the globe and the thought of just coming back to my life the way it is is harrowing. I’ve figured out the solution for me is to just work on things I care about (and obviously be able to pay my bills and live etc.) and if that means I got to put elbow grease, I am happy to do so.

    In the past, I’ve been allergic to the idea of work. I recognise I’ve been influenced by r/antiwork and being bullied as a kid for being “too eager” etc. which has got me questioning my own work ethic so I wind up pulling back when I could go forward. I have realised that it’s that mindset which single handedly fucks me up. I’m very glad to see things move with more work thrown at it. It brings me confidence in seeing this out.

    Next week, my goal is to really up my activity. The book has some nice activity benchmarks so imma try hit it.
